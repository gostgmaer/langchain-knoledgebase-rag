Remote and Hybrid Work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Everyone is expected to be reachable during core hours, 10:00 to 15:00 in their local time zone. New hires spend their first month fully on site. A one-time home office stipend of 500 dollars is paid after the probation period for a desk, chair or monitor. Working from another country for more than 14 days requires approval from HR and legal.
