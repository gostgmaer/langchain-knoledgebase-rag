Expense Reimbursement Policy

Business expenses under 50 dollars do not need a receipt. Any expense of 50 dollars or more must be supported by an itemised receipt. Claims must be submitted in the finance tool within 30 days of the purchase, otherwise they are rejected. Meals while working away from home are reimbursed up to 60 dollars per day. Alcohol is never reimbursable. Reimbursement is paid with the next monthly payroll run.
