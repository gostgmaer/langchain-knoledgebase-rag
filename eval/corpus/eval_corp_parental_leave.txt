Parental Leave Policy

New parents, including adoptive parents, are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be taken in one block or split into two blocks within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption. Parental leave is separate from annual leave and does not reduce the annual leave balance. Employees returning from parental leave may request a phased return of up to 4 weeks at reduced hours without loss of pay.
