Usage trends for this tenant: queries per day, feedback over time, and the most-flagged responses.
Switch the range — **7 days**, **30 days**, **90 days** — at the top of the page.

- **Queries per day** — a line chart of how many questions were asked each day in the selected
  range.
- **Feedback trend** — a bar chart of thumbs up vs. thumbs down per day, so you can see whether
  satisfaction is trending up or down, not just the raw count.
- **Top failing queries** — the assistant responses that collected the most negative feedback,
  ranked by times flagged. The ones most worth reading first if answer quality is slipping; see
  [Feedback](/docs/feedback) for every individual rating behind these numbers.

Until there's chat activity and feedback for this tenant, the page shows an empty state instead of
blank charts.
