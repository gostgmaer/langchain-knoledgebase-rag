Everyone's personal account settings, regardless of role:

- **Profile** — first/last/display name and phone number. Display name is shown instead of your
  email wherever there's room for it.
- **Password** — change the password you sign in with. Requires your current password, and a new
  one at least 8 characters long, entered twice to confirm.
- **Connected accounts** — link or unlink Google, Microsoft or Facebook sign-in, wherever your
  administrator has turned them on. Signing in with a provider that confirms your email address
  connects it automatically the first time; **Connect** otherwise opens that provider's own sign-in
  flow, and **Disconnect** removes the link (your password still works afterward).
