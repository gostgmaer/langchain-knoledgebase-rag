Token consumption and estimated cost for this tenant, over the same **7/30/90-day** range toggle as
[Analytics](/docs/analytics).

- **Prompt tokens**, **Completion tokens**, **Total tokens** — summed across the selected range.
- **Total cost** — an estimate based on those token counts; shown to six decimal places when it's
  under a cent, so a handful of cheap queries doesn't just round down to "$0.00".
- **Daily token usage** — a line chart of total tokens per day, for spotting spikes rather than
  just reading the period total.
